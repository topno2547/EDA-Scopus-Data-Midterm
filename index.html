Keyword Analytics Dashboard

Comprehensive multi-dimensional analysis of the Computer Science Scopus dataset, visualizing trends, popularity, and thematic relationships.

1. Keyword Treemap (สัดส่วนความนิยม)

A quick overview of keyword popularity. Larger and darker boxes represent words that appear most frequently in research.

2. Keyword Theme River (กระแสเทรนด์)

Analyzes the evolution of the top 10 most popular keywords over time. Thickness indicates the volume of articles each year.

3. Keyword Trend Heatmap (ตารางความหนาแน่น)

Detailed density and growth rate of keywords for each year.

4. Emerging Keyword Quadrant (วิเคราะห์ดาวรุ่ง)

Analyzes emerging keywords versus established ones. The X-axis is the growth rate, and the Y-axis is the current frequency.

5. Keyword Co-occurrence Network (เครือข่ายความสัมพันธ์)

Relationship network of keywords. Colors indicate the average publication year (warm tones = recent, cool tones = older). Use the dropdown to highlight specific terms.
